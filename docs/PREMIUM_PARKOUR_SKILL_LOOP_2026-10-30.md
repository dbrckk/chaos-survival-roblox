# Chaos Survival — Premium platformer skill loop (30 October 2026)

## Target

Make the five large survival arenas feel worth mastering as physical platformer courses, not just impressive scenery. This package adds a **complete** parkour reward loop on top of existing 100ms coyote jumping, 120ms buffered jumps, physical routes, 2K PBR, danger telegraphs and deterministic rookie coaching.

**No Roblox public update** is authorized or triggered by this feature. The last verified public version remains the successful 30 October release of `a0f56bcd4cfa401162a608404c95649bb39b6446`.

## End-to-end implementation

1. **Actual physical jump validation — `ParkourFlow`.** The server observes human `Humanoid.StateChanged` and `FloorMaterial` transitions (no Heartbeat loops, no client messages). Once the humanoid reports ground contact, it raycasts downward with collision respected and the character excluded. Only an anchored, collidable, uncollapsed, opaque server-owned `Platform#` inside the ACTIVE arena's `Platforms` folder can register. The character root must be inside the genuine CFrame footprint and plausible standing height. Cosmetic structures, stale maps, collapsed pads and the main floor are not credited. **Authenticity hardening:** a linked landing now requires an actual server-observed `Jumping` state from the source deck, a near-deck takeoff origin within the coyote edge margin, 0.2–3.0 seconds of flight and plausible horizontal travel (at most 48 studs/s plus sampling allowance). Landing on the same deck consumes that flight, so walking across a nearby seam afterwards cannot cash in the jump. A player already on a deck when READY ends receives an initial physical contact sample.
2. **Exact course progression.** Only an actual successor accepted by `JumpRouteCueRules.nextPhysical` is a successful link. At authored perimeter stages 5/6, either legitimate physically jumpable branch/crosslink is also accepted. Stage transitions must occur between **0.27 and 9.0 seconds** apart; returning to an older deck, exiting onto the floor or changing arena breaks the CURRENT chain. The BEST chain for the round is retained. No teleport, modified jump power or artificially added collision. Perimeter routes support up to 7 chained jumps across eight platforms; Harbor offers 6 across seven quays.
3. **Visible feel, rather than GPS spam.** Once two real jumps are chained, the EXISTING lower round focus card displays a slim, localized jump-chain badge anchored above it. The badge disappears for low-end Android Low tier, for final rush and for high-ground mission HUD priority. No extra RenderStepped, remotes, world geometry or GPU-heavy bloom.
4. **Meaningful but capped rewards.** A verified best chain of 3 earns the **PARKOUR ACE** medal and **4 XP**. Six or more earns **RIDGE LEGEND** too, with a total of **7 XP** (not additional unlimited grants). XP is added once in the authoritative per-player end-of-round settlement, including a round where the player later dies; challenge coins, purchases, DataStores and ordinary survival payouts remain unchanged. No client can call an endpoint to claim an earned jump.
5. **Persistent personal best.** `DataSchema` v9 adds a bounded 0–7 `ParkourBestRecord`, automatically migrated to zero for existing player saves and normalized against NaN/Infinity/outliers. On end-of-round settlement, only a better verified run increases the saved record, through the existing DataStore ownership guard. The existing lobby progress billboard gains a small localized `BEST ×N` / `RECORD ×N` label rather than a new screen or 3D model.
6. **Honest results and analytics.** The round receipt includes `parkourBest`, `parkourXP`, both localized medal names and the actual increased XP total; EN/FR detailed XP and jump-chain tags are shown. Degraded/no-save sessions still allow cosmetic medals but never claim an unpersisted XP award. One analytics event after a real XP payout helps study whether players climb higher and replay more.

## Performance and lifecycle

- Two event connections PER human participant only while alive in a round; both disconnected through the existing `RoundCleanup` callbacks, no extra permanent watchers.
- No new per-frame query. A single physics ray is attempted only on landed/ground material transitions; duplicate landings on the same physical Part never increase the chain. The sampler handles both signals immediately instead of delaying 100ms and missing rapid expert re-jumps.
- Engine specs added to the dedicated `parkour-flow.spec.luau` and existing `round-medals.spec.luau` / `data-schema.spec.luau`: full eight-platform route, stale/invalid/ghost deck, time bounds, duplicate contacts, capped rewards, medals and safe schema v9 migration. No tests are run during this ongoing improvement phase, per owner rules.

## Jump authenticity and hazard-guidance revision (same unpublished premium candidate)

This pass closes a discovered integrity bug: previously a character crossing from one server-owned `Platform#` Part onto its physically adjacent successor could earn a chained "jump" **without taking off**. Now both the takeoff and the destination must be server-observed, the originating foot position must be within a narrow real-deck/coyote margin and the flight displacement must be plausible. No double-jumps, arbitrary speed changes, remote score inputs, or force impulses are added.

At the same time, `JumpRouteCueRules.safeGuidedPath` checks authentic server-owned `GridSweepBand#` and `HarborSweepLane#` Parts during `Warning` and `Pulse`. It clips the path through each real band CFrame, including the server's floor-only Y range; raised walkways safely clear the pulse. The client reads only the four direct `Mechanics` children at its existing 0.40/0.85 s cue sampling cadence, with no RenderStepped / Humanoid movement writes. The isolated `parkour-flow.spec.luau` covers all these cases for the next major QA milestone; these tests have **not yet been run for this latest unpublished revision**.
