# Community, Trust & Player Safety Department Review

## Specialist mindset

Review as:
- community manager;
- trust/safety product designer;
- moderation-aware game designer;
- player support lead.

## Purpose

A premium multiplayer game includes the social environment around the mechanics.

## Social safety principles

- no mechanic should require harassment or targeting another player;
- status systems should not humiliate low-skill/new players;
- failure messages should avoid blame;
- AI Survivors must never be presented as real people;
- social features should be opt-in and low-pressure.

## Player identity

Cosmetics/status:
- celebrate mastery/identity;
- do not imply real-money superiority;
- avoid misleading "exclusive" pressure where unnecessary.

## Chat/dependency rule

Core gameplay must not require chat.

A player who mutes/ignores chat should still:
- understand vote;
- understand hazard;
- play normally;
- react socially through safe lightweight systems.

## Social interaction design

Preferred interactions:
- GG;
- Again;
- celebratory emote;
- shared result;
- invite/share.

Avoid mechanics that reward:
- spam;
- insults;
- sabotaging teammates;
- exclusion.

## Reporting/support readiness

Before public scale, documentation should state:
- where players report bugs;
- where purchase problems are escalated;
- how data-loss reports are handled;
- how reproducible exploit reports are triaged.

## Abuse/threat review

Think through:
- remote spam;
- invite spam;
- share spam;
- chat harassment around status;
- offensive user-generated display names;
- griefing using collision/body blocking if applicable.

Use Roblox platform moderation where appropriate; do not duplicate platform systems unnecessarily.

## Privacy/data discipline

Analytics should collect game behavior needed for product decisions, not unnecessary personal information.

Avoid putting sensitive/user-authored data into analytics labels.

## Community scorecard /10

- social pressure;
- newcomer dignity;
- abuse resistance;
- safe non-chat interaction;
- support readiness;
- transparency;
- privacy discipline;
- fair status systems.
