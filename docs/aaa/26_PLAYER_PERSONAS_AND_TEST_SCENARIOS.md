# Player Research — Personas & Test Scenarios

These personas are test lenses, not stereotypes or marketing segments.

## Persona A — First-time mobile player

Knows Roblox controls generally but knows nothing about Chaos Survival.

Test:
- first 60 seconds;
- first vote;
- first READY;
- first death/survival.

Success:
- understands core goal without external explanation;
- does not open unnecessary menus during danger;
- can identify why they died.

## Persona B — Low-end Android player

Prioritizes responsiveness over visual fidelity.

Test:
- Low VFX;
- 30–60 minute soak;
- Double Chaos;
- result;
- AI population.

Success:
- stable floor performance;
- critical telegraphs preserved;
- no UI overlap.

## Persona C — Solo player

Joins an empty server.

Test:
- lobby liveliness;
- AI credibility;
- vote;
- full session loop;
- spectator/result.

Success:
- never feels blocked by lack of humans;
- AI makes world feel alive without pretending to be human.

## Persona D — Friend pair

Joins specifically to play together.

Test:
- invite/share arrival;
- lobby recognition;
- shared rounds;
- reactions;
- crew continuity.

Success:
- social system reinforces shared story without interrupting gameplay.

## Persona E — Experienced/high-skill player

Understands hazards and seeks mastery.

Test:
- repeated sessions;
- Double Chaos;
- risk/reward;
- mastery;
- route optimization.

Success:
- skill still matters;
- content does not become trivial;
- difficulty comes from decisions, not obscurity.

## Persona F — Low-motion / accessibility user

Uses ReduceMotion and possibly muted audio.

Success:
- information parity;
- comfortable camera;
- no essential lost cue.

## Persona G — Cosmetic/progression player

Enjoys collection more than leaderboard-style status.

Success:
- goals are understandable;
- cosmetics feel desirable;
- no P2W pressure;
- progression does not overwhelm survival.

## Persona H — Content creator / spectator mindset

Looks for visually legible moments.

Success:
- gameplay tells a story in a short clip;
- arena/hazard recognizable;
- HUD does not obscure the key moment;
- result is screenshotable.

## Test rule

Every milestone should be evaluated against at least:
A, B, C, D and E.

Accessibility changes add F.
Progression/economy changes add G.
Visual/social/public-presentation changes add H.
