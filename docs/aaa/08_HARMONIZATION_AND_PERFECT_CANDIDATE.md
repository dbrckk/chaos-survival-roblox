# Department Review 08 — Final Harmonization & Perfect-Candidate Protocol

## Purpose

This phase begins only after individual departments believe their work is complete.

It is intentionally different from normal development.

Normal development asks:
**What should we improve/add?**

Harmonization asks:
**What conflicts, duplicates, overstates, distracts or feels inconsistent now that everything exists together?**

## Pass 1 — Product coherence

Review entire session:
join → lobby → vote → READY → round → final rush → result → return.

Look for:
- redundant explanation;
- duplicate celebration;
- timing gaps;
- repeated CTA;
- conflicting colors;
- too many progression messages;
- transitions that feel like different games.

## Pass 2 — Visual coherence

Compare:
- four arenas;
- all 11 disasters;
- lobby;
- result;
- cosmetics.

Normalize:
- world scale;
- emissive intensity;
- material fidelity;
- shadow quality;
- warning color hierarchy;
- motion amplitude;
- density of detail.

Do not make arenas identical. Equalize quality, not identity.

## Pass 3 — Motion coherence

Compare:
- player procedural motion;
- authored clips;
- bots;
- landmarks;
- VFX;
- UI transitions.

Question:
Does everything move with a compatible sense of weight and tempo?

## Pass 4 — Audio coherence

Normalize:
- loudness;
- dynamic range;
- cue duration;
- warning priority;
- reward intensity;
- arena ambience.

Avoid a single legacy sound making an otherwise premium scene feel cheap.

## Pass 5 — UX coherence

Create a screen-state matrix:
- first join;
- lobby;
- vote;
- ready;
- round;
- final rush;
- eliminated/spectator;
- result;
- settings/meta.

For each state list every visible UI element.

Delete or demote anything without a current purpose.

## Pass 6 — Difficulty coherence

Compare survival rates and perceived difficulty across:
- all disasters;
- all arenas;
- solo/multi;
- Double Chaos.

The hardest content may be hard, but it must be intentionally hard.

## Pass 7 — Progression/economy coherence

Ask:
- too many reward channels?
- duplicate currencies?
- rewards meaningful?
- mastery pace appropriate?
- cosmetics desirable enough?
- paid cosmetics distinct but fair?

Simplify where possible.

## Pass 8 — Performance coherence

Profile the **complete** game, not isolated systems.

Find interactions:
- result constellation + lighting + cosmetics;
- Double Chaos + arena atmosphere;
- AI + VFX + touch UI;
- multiple rounds + cleanup.

## Pass 9 — Accessibility coherence

Verify every new layer respects:
- ReduceMotion;
- Low VFX;
- muted audio;
- color independence;
- minimum text size.

## Pass 10 — Social coherence

Observe a real group:
- do players notice one another?
- do they react after a shared event?
- does invite/share appear at a natural moment?
- does social UI interrupt the next round?

## Pass 11 — Subtraction review

For every major system ask:
"If removed, what player value disappears?"

If answer is weak, remove or merge it.

A premium product is edited.

## Pass 12 — Outside-the-box audit

Assign temporary specialist perspectives:

### Film editor
Where is pacing too slow/fast?

### Theme-park designer
Where does attention naturally go in physical space?

### Sports-broadcast director
Are stakes, status and climax visible to spectators?

### Industrial designer
Do machines/materials look constructed for a purpose?

### Sociologist
What creates belonging, status, norms and shared ritual?

### Behavioral scientist
Are motivation loops healthy or manipulative?

### Accessibility specialist
What assumption excludes a player?

### Mobile hardware engineer
What is wasting battery/frame time?

### Security researcher
What can a malicious client lie about?

### New player
What is unexplained?

### Expert player
What becomes repetitive?

### Content creator
What moments are visually understandable in a 3-second clip?

Record at least one observation from each lens.

## Perfect-Candidate decision

All must be true:
- specialist scorecards meet target;
- no known P0/P1;
- CI green;
- Open Cloud green;
- Studio E2E green;
- physical Android/desktop gates complete;
- persistence/rejoin proven;
- public alpha reviewed;
- art/audio asset licensing known;
- storefront prepared from real gameplay;
- no duplicated major systems;
- harmonization completed;
- subtraction review completed.

Then create:
- RC tag;
- exact changelog;
- rollback reference;
- final screenshots;
- final device report.

## After Perfect-Candidate

Stop broad improvement work.

Only:
- fix verified issues;
- respond to playtest/analytics;
- prepare release;
- monitor production.

Perfection at this stage means:
**no known meaningful improvement has a better cost/benefit than shipping and observing real players.**
