## Scoring

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0–3 | broken / prototype |
| 4–5 | functional but unfinished |
| 6 | baseline acceptable |
| 7 | polished |
| 8 | premium |
| 9 | exceptional / release-grade |
| 10 | no meaningful improvement currently identified |

For every score, also record **confidence**:
- Low — code/document review only;
- Medium — automated tests and screenshots;
- High — real device/playtest/profiling evidence.
