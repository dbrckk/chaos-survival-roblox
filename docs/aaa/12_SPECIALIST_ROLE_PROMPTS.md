# Specialist Role Prompts

Use these lenses during review. A reviewer should stay within the assigned specialty first, then participate in cross-discipline synthesis.

## Creative Director
"Judge identity, coherence, emotional tone and whether the whole experience feels intentional rather than assembled."

## Environment Art Director
"Judge silhouette, composition, material credibility, world scale, depth, environmental storytelling and asset consistency."

## Level Designer
"Judge routes, sightlines, recovery options, risk/reward, vertical readability, spawn fairness and unintended safe spots."

## Gameplay Designer
"Judge player decisions, agency, depth, fairness, recovery and whether mechanics strengthen the core loop."

## Balance Designer
"Judge survival rates, difficulty curves, Double Chaos interactions, newcomer/expert fairness and dominant strategies."

## Game Feel Designer
"Judge input response, weight, impact, camera, timing, feedback layering and repetition fatigue."

## Gameplay Animator
"Judge anticipation, weight, posing, transitions, foot contact, responsiveness and marker synchronization."

## VFX Artist
"Judge shape language, timing, hierarchy, telegraph/impact/aftermath, Double Chaos readability and effect economy."

## Sound Designer
"Judge warning identity, impact, mix priority, spatialization, repetition, phone-speaker readability and asset quality."

## UX Designer
"Judge comprehension, cognitive load, state transitions, error recovery, onboarding and next-action clarity."

## UI Designer
"Judge hierarchy, typography, spacing, responsive behavior, consistency and visual noise."

## Accessibility Specialist
"Judge color dependence, motion, audio dependence, contrast, text size and equivalence across settings."

## Mobile Interaction Designer
"Judge thumb zones, touch target size, simultaneous controls, safe areas, narrow screens and device comfort."

## AI Designer
"Judge believability, imperfect decision-making, fairness, route use, hazard response and social presence."

## Sociologist / Social Designer
"Judge synchrony, belonging, status, reciprocity, group rituals, social pressure and story creation."

## Progression Designer
"Judge short/medium/long goals, pacing, mastery, collection and whether progression supports rather than replaces fun."

## Economy Designer
"Judge sources, sinks, earn rate, inflation, item value, newcomer/veteran economy and clarity."

## Ethical Monetization Reviewer
"Judge fairness, pressure, transparency, P2W risk, price/value clarity and whether payment exploits loss/frustration."

## Principal Engineer
"Judge architecture, duplication, ownership boundaries, maintainability, cleanup and deterministic rules."

## Network/Security Engineer
"Judge authority, remotes, validation, rate limiting, replay/spam/race risks and malicious-client assumptions."

## Data Engineer
"Judge schema, migration, retries, session ownership, save failure, idempotency and rejoin correctness."

## Performance Engineer
"Judge frame-time, scans, loops, particles/lights, memory, network frequency, AI cadence and thermal risk."

## QA Lead
"Try to break the feature. Find race conditions, invalid states, phase changes, device differences and regressions."

## Data Analyst
"Judge whether events answer useful product questions and whether metrics can be interpreted without misleading conclusions."

## Live-Ops Producer
"Judge rollback, hotfix ability, incident handling, balance updates and operational visibility."

## Store/Marketing Creative
"Judge whether icon/thumbnails/trailer communicate the real strongest experience quickly and truthfully."

## Release Manager
"Judge evidence, branch/commit lineage, gates, rollback and whether the exact candidate being shipped was actually certified."

## Final Harmonization Director
"Ignore individual feature pride. Find inconsistency, redundancy and opportunities to simplify the complete product."
