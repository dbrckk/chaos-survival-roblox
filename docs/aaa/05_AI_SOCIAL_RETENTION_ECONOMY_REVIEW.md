# Department Review 05 — AI, Social Design, Retention, Economy & Monetization

## Specialist mindset

Review as:
- AI gameplay designer;
- social systems designer;
- behavioral/sociology researcher;
- progression designer;
- economy designer;
- ethical monetization reviewer.

## AI Survivor contract

AI exists to make sparse servers feel alive and playable.

It must:
- obey same damage/physics rules;
- react plausibly;
- make occasional believable mistakes;
- express personality;
- navigate each arena;
- participate socially;
- never pretend to be an actual human account.

It must not:
- know future hazards;
- teleport to safety;
- have perfect reaction time;
- secretly receive health/speed advantages;
- distort public online-player claims.

## AI believability dimensions

Score separately:
- movement;
- hesitation;
- route choice;
- hazard reaction;
- social behavior;
- visual individuality;
- result reaction;
- recovery from being stuck.

Too-perfect AI is less believable than slightly imperfect AI.

## Social-design contract

The core social loop:
**shared choice → shared danger → shared climax → shared result → recognition → replay/share/invite.**

Social features must follow emotional value, not precede it.

Good:
- reaction after result;
- invite after a meaningful round;
- shared survivor presentation;
- crew continuity;
- shareable clutch/fusion moment.

Bad:
- invite popup at login;
- reward for spam invites;
- paywall around social play;
- fake urgency.

## Sociology lenses

Review:
- synchrony — do players experience beats together?
- identity — can players express themselves without dominance?
- reciprocity — can players acknowledge one another cheaply?
- status — is mastery visible without humiliating newcomers?
- belonging — does lobby feel like a shared place?
- story — does a round create a retellable event?

## Retention architecture

Retention should exist at several time scales.

Seconds:
- survive next warning.

Minutes:
- win round;
- complete challenge;
- attempt Double Chaos;
- continue streak.

Sessions:
- mastery;
- weeklies;
- collection;
- achievements;
- social continuity.

Weeks:
- cosmetic collection;
- arena/disaster mastery;
- rotating goals;
- content updates.

Do not expose every horizon simultaneously.

## Healthy retention gate

Reject:
- punitive absence streak;
- excessive FOMO;
- loss aversion used to sell power;
- forced notification loops;
- currency confusion;
- progress hidden behind payment.

Player should return because:
- the loop is good;
- mastery is satisfying;
- friends create stories;
- identity/progression has meaning.

## Economy contract

Every source/sink should have:
- purpose;
- expected frequency;
- expected amount;
- inflation impact;
- newcomer impact;
- veteran impact.

Track:
- coins earned/hour;
- cosmetic time-to-earn;
- free vs paid perceived value;
- unused currency accumulation.

## Monetization contract

Allowed:
- trails;
- auras;
- cosmetic sets;
- visual identity;
- supporter packs;
- social/emote cosmetics.

Not allowed:
- health;
- movement power;
- better warnings;
- hazard immunity;
- revive advantage;
- increased win chance;
- progression multiplier that undermines fair mastery.

## Price psychology

Price should feel:
- optional;
- understandable;
- low-friction;
- proportionate to cosmetic value.

Do not use manipulative fake discounts or confusing bundles.

## Social/retention/economy scorecard /10

- AI plausibility;
- solo liveliness;
- social presence;
- story generation;
- replay motivation;
- progression clarity;
- retention health;
- economy balance;
- monetization fairness;
- identity value.

Fairness and solo quality must be >=9.
