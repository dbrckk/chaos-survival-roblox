# Cross-Discipline Tool — Red Team & Innovation Backlog

## Purpose

Normal iteration improves what already exists.
This review deliberately changes perspective to find improvements outside the current roadmap.

## Inversion exercises

### Make the game confusing
List everything that would make the game confusing:
- too many overlays;
- same warning color;
- hidden danger;
- long copy;
- camera obstruction.

Then verify none are present.

### Make the game feel cheap
List:
- generic sounds;
- identical materials;
- robotic animation;
- default-looking UI;
- repeated procedural shapes;
- inconsistent easing.

Attack the strongest remaining item.

### Make a player quit after one death
List:
- unexplained cause;
- long wait;
- no revenge path;
- popup monetization;
- spectator boredom.

Eliminate these conditions.

### Make solo feel empty
List:
- motionless lobby;
- silent bots;
- no reactions;
- weak result staging.

Check the current solo session against the list.

## External-profession lenses

### Architect

### Cinematographer
Where does the eye go first?

### Sound-film mixer
What should be heard when everything happens?

### Sports producer
Can a spectator instantly understand stakes and climax?

### Theme-park designer
How is attention guided through space?

### Sociologist

### Community manager

### Content creator

## Current innovation experiments worth evaluating

Not commitments. Test before keeping.

1. **Result composition mode**
   Briefly improve composition for screenshot/share without taking camera control.

2. **Dynamic sensory governor**
   Reduce decorative VFX/audio automatically when multiple high-priority hazards overlap.

3. **Hazard readability telemetry**
   Correlate deaths shortly after first warning with disaster/device/VFX tier.

4. **First-death explanation quality**
   Track whether quit happens immediately after a death and improve the relevant post-death cue.

5. **Adaptive lobby calmness**
   Reduce lobby spectacle after long sessions to fight fatigue.

6. **Authoring-vs-procedural benchmark**
   Replace one animation/hero asset and compare perceived quality before scaling asset production.

7. **Screenshot composition audit**
   Collect canonical frames per arena/phase and review them side by side as an art board.

8. **Audio phone-speaker mix**
   Create a dedicated validation pass for small speakers, not just headphones.

9. **Bot uncanny-valley review**
   Intentionally look for patterns that reveal bots: synchronized movement, instant turns, repeated reactions.

10. **Subtraction sprint**
    One iteration where no feature may be added; only remove duplication/noise/latency.

## Experiment rule

Every experiment needs:
- hypothesis;
- smallest implementation;
- success criterion;
- cost;
- rollback;
- decision date.

If evidence is weak, remove it.
